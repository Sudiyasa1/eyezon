# PERCEPTION — the decision

PERCEPTION is the engine that decides whether a token is worth your attention.
Most are not. That is the entire point.

## What it weighs

**Liquidity depth** — can a position actually be entered and exited, or is the
pool decorative?

**Holder spread** — how concentrated is supply? A handful of wallets holding
most of it can end a chart whenever they choose.

**Buy dominance** — is demand real and two-sided, or manufactured?

**Authenticity** — do the wallets buying look organic, or coordinated?

**Rug safety** — contract-level signatures of the traps that end tokens: live
mint authority, live freeze authority, and the patterns that precede a pull.

## Selectivity is the product

Roughly one in fifteen graduations clears. The other fourteen fail one of the
above. Anyone can forward every launch; the value is in what does not get sent.

## Stamped at the moment of the call

A call posts with its entry market cap recorded at that exact second. It is not
recalculated afterwards, which means a published multiple cannot be flattered by
picking a kinder starting point later.
